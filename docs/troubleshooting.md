# Troubleshooting Case Studies

This page documents real troubleshooting situations I have worked through and what I learned from them.

## Windows PC Boot Loop and Motherboard Beep Codes

### Problem

A desktop PC was stuck in a boot loop and the motherboard was producing beep codes instead of starting normally.

### Investigation

I used the motherboard beep-code pattern as a diagnostic clue and researched what the pattern indicated. The symptoms pointed toward a hardware issue, with RAM being one of the likely causes.

### Troubleshooting steps

1. Shut the PC down completely and disconnected power.
2. Opened the system and checked the memory and nearby hardware connections.
3. Removed and reseated the RAM to make sure it was properly seated.
4. Cleared CMOS as part of the troubleshooting process.
5. Reassembled the system and tested the boot process again.

### Result

The PC booted normally again after the hardware was reseated and the system was re-tested.

### What I learned

- Hardware symptoms such as beep codes can help narrow down a fault before replacing parts.
- It is better to troubleshoot methodically and test likely causes one at a time than to guess.
- Reseating components is a simple but useful first step when a system shows signs of a connection or memory problem.
- Documenting the symptoms and steps taken makes future troubleshooting easier.
